Import daily scanner history

One-off. Writes 155 documents — 31 days of August 2026, five batteries — into tbReadings as DAILY figures with no three-hour readings behind them. Source: SCANNER_READING_today_2.xlsx, sheet MAR Scanner Readings.

Read this before you press the button.

• The four Belayim columns in the sheet are TOTAL GROSS. The T.B. 10/1 column is NET OIL. They are different quantities and the app cannot tell them apart — 10/1 will look low against Belayim for that reason alone, not because it is producing badly.

• On 7 Aug the sheet's own TOTAL GROSS (17,402) does not equal its four batteries added up (17,353), a difference of 49 m³. The per-battery figures are what get imported, so the app will read 17,353 for that day and disagree with the sheet.

• Existing documents are merged, not replaced: any three-hour readings already entered for these days survive, and a day that has real readings will keep using them — the imported figure is only used where there is no reading at all.

• Safe to run twice. It writes the same values again.
